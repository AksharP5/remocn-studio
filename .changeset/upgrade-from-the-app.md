Upgrade from the app. The Upgrade button — on the trial card and in
Settings › Account, and the tooltip Inspect and Snapshot carry on Free —
offers the monthly and the yearly price, asks the account server for a
checkout and opens it in the browser. While the page is open the app asks the
server every five seconds, for ten minutes, then offers Check again; the
purchase ends on one line, *Subscription active*, with the plan already Pro
and no relaunch. A declined card's *Update card* opens the billing portal.
