Delete what you selected on the canvas yourself, without asking the agent: press ⌫ or Delete, use Delete in the properties header, or right-click the object on the canvas or its row in Layers. It disappears at once, and ⌘Z or Undo in the notice brings it back. A managed object is removed in the video's `studio.json` and stops being painted while everything that reads its values keeps working; the first deletion in a video moves it to the `studio-objects-v6` runtime. An element outside the catalogue is removed from its place in the code; one line that draws several instances offers Delete all N. Scenes and objects not on screen cannot be deleted, and say why.
